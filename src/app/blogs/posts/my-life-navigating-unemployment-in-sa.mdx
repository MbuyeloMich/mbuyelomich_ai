Finding work in South Africa can take time, but this season has also become a chance to grow with purpose. I am learning, building projects, and meeting people who remind me that progress can happen before the perfect opportunity arrives.

The path into work is not the same for everyone. Where opportunities are based, access to transport and reliable internet, and the experience employers expect can all shape the journey. Recognizing those challenges helps me focus on the steps I can take while staying open to support, collaboration, and new possibilities.

## Turning learning into momentum

I keep developing practical skills and putting them to use in projects. Each build gives me a way to test an idea, solve a real problem, and show what I can contribute. Learning is most rewarding when it moves beyond tutorials and becomes something useful that other people can try.

I do not see every skill or project as a guarantee of employment. I see them as progress: stronger foundations, clearer examples of my work, and more confidence explaining how I approach a challenge.

## Building with other people

Hackathons have become an important part of that journey. They bring people together to share ideas, learn from different perspectives, and turn a concept into a working prototype under time pressure. The experience can lead to new connections and sharper technical skills, but just as importantly, it reminds me that building does not have to happen alone.

Projects like Hlokomela, our IoT water-intelligence platform, show me what a team can do when it combines technical thinking with a problem that matters to communities. Experiences like these help me keep growing as an engineer and make my work feel connected to something beyond a portfolio.

## Making room for opportunity

I continue to apply for roles, improve my work, and connect with people in the tech community. I am also learning to value consistency without expecting myself to be productive every moment. Rest, asking for advice, and taking time to reflect all help me return with perspective.

There is no single route into a career, and a delayed opportunity does not erase the effort already made. Every project completed, lesson learned, and conversation shared adds to the path I am building.

## Moving forward with hope

Unemployment is part of my current experience, not the limit of what I can become. I am staying curious, continuing to build, and looking forward to the opportunity to bring that work into a team where I can contribute and keep learning.

I hope more young people can access the mentorship, networks, and first chances that make the transition into work easier. Until then, I will keep showing up, sharing what I learn, and building toward a future where effort can meet opportunity.
